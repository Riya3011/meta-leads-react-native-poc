Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled by the Meta Lead Ads React Native Proof of Concept.

Information We Collect

When a user submits a lead form, the application may receive information provided through the form, such as the user's name and email address.

How We Use Information

The information received from a submitted lead is used only to demonstrate and test the lead retrieval and real-time display functionality of this Proof of Concept.

Data Sharing

This Proof of Concept does not sell or otherwise use lead information for advertising or commercial purposes.

Data Retention

Test lead information is used only for demonstration and development purposes and is not intended for permanent storage.

Contact

For questions about this Proof of Concept, please contact the project developer through the associated GitHub repository.
